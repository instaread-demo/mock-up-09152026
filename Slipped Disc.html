There is unrest in the music department at Clifton Cathedral in Bristol.

The Roman Catholic congregation has been told that David Bednall has stepped down as Choral Director of the cathedral choir. The cathedral thanked him for his service and his contribution to its musical life.

The post of Choral Director will be advertised nationally this autumn, with an appointment expected in January 2027. In the interim, Harry Kirby — a choral scholar at Clifton since 2018 — has been appointed Acting Choral Director and directs the choir from Sunday 13 September.
