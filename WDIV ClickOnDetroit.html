Westland parish pushes back against proposal that could end weekend Masses

Parishioners have started a petition with more than 400 signatures asking the Archdiocese of Detroit to keep the church fully open and active

WESTLAND, Mich. – The community at St. Mary, Cause of Our Joy is resisting a restructuring plan that could end weekend Masses at the Westland parish beginning in July 2027.

The parish currently offers two weekday Masses in addition to its Saturday and Sunday services.

Hundreds of parishioners say they are worried about the future of a church they describe as central to community life, and a petition asking the archdiocese to reconsider has passed 400 signatures.
