VANDALIA — No students were seriously hurt when two Unit 4 school buses collided out of town on Saturday morning, said Carla Phelan-Erdey, the district’s chief communications and community engagement officer.

Members of the Centennial High School marching band were travelling to a competition in O’Fallon when the crash happened near Vandalia, she told The News-Gazette. Additional buses were dispatched to take students home.

Two students sought medical attention as a precaution once they returned to the Champaign area, with no broken bones or other injuries reported. An administrative review of the incident is ongoing.
